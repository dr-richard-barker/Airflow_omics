Computational Fluid Dynamics-Guided Multi-Omics Meta-Analysis of Arabidopsis Spaceflight Adaptation

Integrating RNA-seq and microarray data from 451 spaceflight samples with CFD gas-exchange boundary layer modeling, single-cell VAE deconvolution, and systems biology models of hormone and carbon allocation.

Richard Barker · Arabidopsis thaliana · 451 samples · 16 NASA OSDR studies

Overview

Atmospheric microenvironment, not microgravity alone, drives plant spaceflight responses.

Using a limma-voom mixed-effects model and lattice-Boltzmann CFD simulations of leaf-level gas exchange, we show that growth hardware dominates transcriptomic variation. Sealed BRIC canisters restrict gas exchange and limit photosynthesis to ~1% of Earth rates, inducing a severe boundary-layer penalty that correlates with suppression of auxin, jasmonate, and ABA pathways.
