Airflow Omics · NASA OSDR & CFD

Spaceflight changes plant gene expression, but differently in every experiment

A study-aware re-analysis of 307 Arabidopsis spaceflight transcriptomes from 11 NASA OSDR studies, coupled to lattice-Boltzmann CFD of the BRIC, CARA and VEGGIE growth hardware.

Richard Barker · Arabidopsis thaliana · 307 samples · 11 OSDR studies · re-analysis October 2026

Read the findings Full re-analysis Code & data (GitHub)

Overview

The flight response is real but largely specific to each experiment.

A flight effect shared by all 11 studies explains under 1 % of gene variance, against 7–11 % for study-specific flight effects. Within BRIC hardware, only the RNA-seq studies agree with each other. Neither CFD-predicted boundary-layer conductance nor carbon supply explains which studies respond, and the CFD shows why. Inside a real flight dish, gravity changes leaf gas exchange only 1.25–1.8-fold, and sealed BRIC dishes run short of CO₂ on the ground as well as in orbit.
